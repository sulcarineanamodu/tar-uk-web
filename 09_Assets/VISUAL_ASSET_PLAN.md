## 8. IMAGE NAMING CONVENTION

### Format: `tar-[section]-[subject]-[variant].ext`

**Breakdown:**
- `tar-` = Project prefix
- `[section]` = hero, service, engineer, texture, icon, logo
- `[subject]` = specific item name (washing-machine, lead-portrait, etc.)
- `[variant]` = (optional) mobile, dark, light, lg, etc.
- `.ext` = file extension (jpg, png, svg, gif)

**Examples:**
- `tar-hero-bg-primary.jpg`
- `tar-service-washing-machine.jpg`
- `tar-service-washing-machine-mobile.jpg`
- `tar-engineer-lead-portrait.jpg`
- `tar-icon-washing-machine.svg`
- `tar-texture-grid-overlay.png`
- `tar-logo-mark.png`

## 11. COLOR & STYLE GUIDELINES FOR IMAGES

### 11.1 Photography Style
- **Lighting:** Professional, warm white balance (3200-4000K)
- **Depth of Field:** Shallow (f/2.8-f/5.6) to focus on subject
- **Shadows:** Soft, no harsh black shadows (use fill light)
- **Highlights:** Slight overexposure acceptable for premium feel
- **Saturation:** Natural, not oversaturated
- **Contrast:** High but natural (not crushed blacks)

### 11.2 Color Tone
- **Primary Colors in Image:** Navy, charcoal, deep grays
- **Accent Colors:** Electric blue (#0ea5e9) in fixtures or lighting
- **Secondary Accent:** Subtle yellow (#fbbf24) in highlights or tools
- **Background:** Blurred modern interiors, professional workshops
- **No Bright Primary Reds:** Avoid generic "repair" red iconography

### 11.3 Composition
- **Subject Placement:** Rule of thirds (subject off-center)
- **Backgrounds:** Blurred, neutral, professional
- **Framing:** Close-ups showing detail and expertise
- **Action:** Show technician working, not just product
- **Branding:** Subtle (no large company logos/text on subject)

### 11.4 Editing & Post-Processing
- **Tone:** Cool-ish (slight blue cast to match brand)
- **Clarity:** Slightly increased (professional pop)
- **Vibrance:** Natural, not oversaturated
- **Vignette:** Subtle (darkened edges for focus)
- **Grain:** Optional film grain for cinematic feel
- **Sharpness:** High (professional photography standard)

## 12. PRODUCTION TIMELINE & SOURCING

### 12.1 Image Sourcing Options

**Option A: Professional Photography (Recommended)**
- Hire professional photographer (1-2 day shoot)
- Cost: £1,500-£3,000
- Quality: Premium, bespoke
- Timeline: 2-3 weeks (including retouching)
- Best for: Hero images, team portraits, engineer action shots

**Option B: High-Quality Stock + Custom Photography**
- Purchase premium stock images (Adobe Stock, Shutterstock)
- Professional photographer for engineer portraits & action
- Cost: £500-£1,500
- Quality: Good, mix of custom & premium stock
- Timeline: 1-2 weeks

**Option C: Premium Stock Only**
- Premium stock library (iStock, Adobe Stock)
- Cost: £300-£800
- Quality: Excellent curated selection available
- Timeline: 1 week
- Works if engineer/team images are not essential

### 12.2 Recommended Approach
**Hybrid Approach:**
- 1-2 hero images: Professional photography
- Engineer portrait: Professional photographer or premium stock
- Service images: Premium stock library (iStock, Getty Images)
- Textures & icons: Design team creation (in-house)
- Logo: Already designed (gradient T mark)

## 14. OPTIMIZATION REQUIREMENTS

### 14.1 File Size & Format
- **JPG:** 70-85% quality compression
- **PNG:** Lossy compression where possible
- **SVG:** Minified, optimized paths
- **Responsive:** Srcset for multiple densities (1x, 2x)

### 14.2 Performance
- Hero image: Max 500 KB (desktop), 300 KB (mobile)
- Service images: Max 250 KB (desktop), 150 KB (mobile)
- Total assets: < 5 MB homepage load

### 14.3 Formats
- Modern: WEBP with JPG fallback
- PNG for transparency
- SVG for icons (scalable)
- ICO + PNG for favicon
